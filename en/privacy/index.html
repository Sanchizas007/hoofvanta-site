Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

In short

Hoofvanta is a logbook. Everything you enter — horses, owners, barns, visits, photos, invoices and payments — is stored on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. There is no account and the developer has no server that receives your logbook.

What the app stores on your iPhone

Your logbook: names, contact details, notes, dates, prices, photos and the settings you choose (language, currency, business name, reminders). It never leaves the phone unless you export it yourself.

Purchases

Hoofvanta is sold as a subscription through Apple. Apple takes the payment and tells the app whether you are subscribed; the developer does not see your payment details.

The app also uses RevenueCat, a subscription service, to record purchases. RevenueCat receives your purchase receipt under an anonymous app user ID, so the developer can see subscription statistics. It is not linked to your name or to your logbook.

Apple Ads attribution

Once per install the app asks Apple for an attribution token (AdServices) and gives it to RevenueCat, which asks Apple which Apple Ads campaign, if any, led to the install. The token carries no personal data. Hoofvanta does not read the advertising identifier, does not ask for permission to track you, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you tap to take a photo of a hoof or a horse; the pictures you choose from your library are copied into the app's storage. Photos are not uploaded anywhere. They are part of a backup file only when you create one.

Reminders

Reminders are notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They need no server, and you can switch them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Backups and exports

Backup files, spreadsheets and PDF invoices are made on your iPhone and go where you send them. Once you share a file, the privacy policy of the app or person you shared it with applies.

Children

Hoofvanta is made for professionals and is not directed at children. The app collects no personal data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes the logbook stored on your iPhone. Your purchase history stays with Apple; to have RevenueCat delete the anonymous record of your purchases, write to the address below.
